# Diagnosed Firmware Bugs

Four bugs were diagnosed against the original firmware. As of the current patched revision, the dead-motor bug is fixed on the running machine; the other three are slated for the ISR-based rebuild (see `rebuild-architecture.md`).

## 1. Dead motor after long pause — FIXED (patched, confirmed on machine)

`StepperAxis::update()` compared `(int32_t)(nowUs - nextStepUs) >= 0` to decide whether a step was due. `stopImmediate()` zeroed `intervalUs` but left `nextStepUs` frozen. Since `micros()` wraps every ~71.58 minutes, the signed difference goes negative for ~35.79 minutes after a wrap, silently halting pulses from both axes.

- **Field evidence:** turn count frozen at 4062, `rpm_now` ramping down from 471→208, `trav_steps` frozen, all other health signals reporting normal.
- **Confirmation test:** `resetall` (calls `begin()`, reseeding `nextStepUs`) restored motion immediately.
- **Self-heals** after ~36 minutes even without intervention.
- **Fix:** idle-freeze `micros()`-wrap re-seed patch, shipped in the 2026-09-16 firmware revision.

## 2. High-RPM stall — OPEN (rebuild required)

Step pulses are generated by polling `micros()` inside `loop()`. Several things can block that loop for 200–600 µs at a time:

- Telemetry bursts
- ~55 µs blocking HX711 reads
- Blocking traverse pulses issued inside the winder's own step handler
- `String` parsing that touches the heap/stack

At 1000 RPM (26.7 kHz step rate → 37.5 µs/step budget), any of these is fatal — the firmware is open-loop, so a stall doesn't halt anything visibly; turns and traverse position keep incrementing as if nothing happened.

- **Fix (planned):** move step generation to a PIT `IntervalTimer` ISR, decoupled from `loop()` entirely. See `rebuild-architecture.md`.

## 3. Traverse drift — OPEN (rebuild required)

Carriage drifts 1–2 mm toward the top per ~2000 turns; currently reset by periodic recalibration.

**Root cause:** DIR setup time is exactly at the 5 µs minimum at each reversal, driven through a 3.3 V opto. The first step pulse after a reversal executes in the *old* direction before the new DIR value is honored, at ~20 µm per affected reversal, always in the same sense. This predicts drift of roughly `20/W` mm per 2000 turns, where W is the winding band width in mm.

Minor contributing factors:
- `update()` counts position on the falling edge of the step pulse
- `stopImmediate()` drops an already-accepted pulse

- **Fix (planned):** 74AHCT125 buffer + writing DIR one full ISR tick before the first step + counting position on the driver's active edge. See drift diagnostics/correction hierarchy in `rebuild-architecture.md`.

## 4. Float ramp precision loss — OPEN (rebuild required)

Field log shows an effective ramp rate of ~65 rpm/s against a configured 75 rpm/s. Repeatedly adding `75e-6` to a `float` accumulator near 471 loses precision at that magnitude.

- **Fix (planned):** replace with a milli-RPM fixed-point ramp evaluated in the 1 kHz control tick.
